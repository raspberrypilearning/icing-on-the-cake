## Make a cake

In this step, you'll add your **cake** sprite.

--- task ---

Create a new sprite. In the costume tab, create your cake. A cake can be any shape or colour you like.

![Choosing a cake sprite in Scratch.](images/choose-cake.png)

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Stamp the cake in the middle of the stage `when green flag clicked`{:class="block3events"}.

```blocks3
when green flag clicked
hide
go to x: (0) y: (0)
stamp
```

--- /task ---

**Test:** Click the green flag and see that the cake appears in the middle of the stage.

**Tip:** Stamping the cake allows the toppings to be drawn on top. If using a sprite, the toppings are drawn behind the cake and you can't see them.
