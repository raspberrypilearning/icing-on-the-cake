## Make different cakes

Your cake-type button needs some different cakes to switch between. In this step, you'll add more **costumes** to the `cake` sprite.

--- task ---

Select the `cake` sprite and click the **Costumes** tab.

Right-click your cake costume and choose **duplicate**, then change the copy into a different cake — a different shape, a taller cake, or a new decoration.

![Duplicating the cake costume and editing it into a different cake.](images/different-cakes.gif)

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Make as many cake costumes as you like. Give each one a clear name.

--- /task ---

--- tip ---

Keep the main colour of every cake costume the **same** as your first cake. Your `toppings` sprite checks for that colour before it stamps, so if you change the colour, stamping will stop working on the new cakes.

--- /tip ---

Your `cake` sprite now has several cakes to choose between. 🎂🍰
